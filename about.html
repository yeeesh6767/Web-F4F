About Filter4Future

Turning outrage into oxygen.

We're a nonprofit collective of scientists, organizers, and neighbors who believe pollution is a solvable problem — not an inevitability.

Our mission

Cut pollution at the source. Restore what's already damaged. Equip communities to protect themselves.

Filter4Future exists to reduce air, water, and plastic pollution in the communities hit hardest by it — often the ones with the fewest resources to fight back. We do this through direct restoration work, low-cost monitoring technology, community education, and policy advocacy.

We measure success in liters of water cleared, tons of CO₂ and particulate matter reduced, and — most importantly — in the health outcomes of the neighborhoods we work in.

What we stand for

Our core values

Integrity

Independently audited finances and public impact reports. Every dollar is traceable.

Community first

Every program is co-designed with the residents it affects — not imposed from outside.

Evidence-driven

We monitor real air and water quality data before, during, and after every project.

Urgency

Pollution harms people today. We favor fast, iterative action over slow perfection.
