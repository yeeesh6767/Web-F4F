What we do

Three fronts. One goal: a livable planet.

We combine grassroots cleanup, ecological restoration, and policy advocacy to attack pollution where it starts — not just where it ends up.

Clean Air Initiative

Deploying low-cost air quality sensors and pushing for emissions accountability in industrial corridors.

River & Coastal Cleanup

Volunteer-powered cleanups and plastic-interception systems on rivers before waste reaches the ocean.

Community Education

School programs and neighborhood workshops that turn awareness into daily habits and local action.
